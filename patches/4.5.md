* **Pop Groups:** Groups track percentages of their Pops in each applicable Ethic and Faction rather than splitting into separate groups for those memberships. Do not infer ideological headcounts from the number of Pop Groups.
* **Faction Movement:** Ethics and Faction membership can shift frequently. Faction attraction responds to promoting or suppressing that Faction, and Factions with the same Ethics can be promoted or suppressed separately.
* **Faction Attraction:** Ethic attraction contributes to Faction growth; Pops have no inherent preference for their current Faction when choosing a Faction. Progressive, Prosperity, and Totalitarian attraction depends on citizenship rights rather than species alone.
* **Cyberization:** Cybernetic Pops gain 10% Job Efficiency from the Cybernetic species trait. The Limited Cybernetic species trait is absent. Under the Limited Cyberization policy, Cybernetic Spiritualist Pops do not benefit from Cybernetic Job Efficiency; the Limited Cyborg leader trait remains.

* **Hedonists:** Their Pop Growth and Assembly contribution scales with their share of a Colony's population, up to +30%. Their Pop Growth requires free Housing and Amenities and disappears during a Consumer Goods deficit.
* **Consecrated Worlds:** Eligible worlds depend on an empire's origin and traits. Consecrating one, two, or three worlds grants deterministic empire-wide bonuses of +10%, +20%, or +40% to Unity, Amenities, and Spiritualist Ethics Attraction.
* **Biomorphosis:** Its completion time is comparable to other ascension paths; do not treat it as unusually slow.
* **Branch Offices:** Building a Branch Office costs 75 Influence plus a distance penalty of 5 Influence per jump.

* **Fleet Doctrine:** The Fleet Doctrine policy offers six doctrines that influence each ship class's tactical role in automated ship designs and designer auto-complete. Weapon and Defense Preference policies also guide automated designs and technology research. Check the empire's selected policies before recommending a specific auto-designed fleet composition.
* **Starbase Preferences:** Starbases have separate Weapon and Defense Preference policies. Starbase, Orbital Ring, and Waystation weapon modules are weapon-type-specific, unlocked by matching weapon technology, and upgrade to the latest available tier.
* **Station Loadouts:** Autocannons, Disruptors, and other short-range weapons cannot be equipped on Starbases, Orbital Rings, or Deep Space Citadels. Automated stations fit armor in locations where shields are negated.
* **AI Fleets:** AI fleet composition follows active fleet doctrine and weapon preference rather than fixed personality and civic ship ratios. Avoid predicting an opponent's composition from its personality alone.
* **Biological Ship Deficits:** Biological ships are affected by Food deficits rather than Energy deficits.
* **Stellar Cannon:** The Stellar Cannon is a Tier 3 technology requiring Wormhole Stabilization and UV Lasers. Do not assume access merely from having a suitable star or Nomads DLC.
* **Operational Reserves:** When a Nomad empire's Operational Reserves are critical, ships and cloned space fauna with a Mineral cost cannot be built.

* **Federation Economic Laws:** Federations can grant member empires free automatic Research Agreements and Commercial Pacts, or Wayline Pacts when Nomads is active. Trade League members receive free automatic Commercial and, with Nomads, Wayline Pacts. Federation members can also form direct Commercial Pacts; non-Trade League members keep existing pacts when joining, while Trade Leagues replace member-to-member pacts with free federation pacts. Account for benefits even when no separately negotiated agreement was made.
* **Joining Wars:** Joining an ongoing war counts as offensive for subject agreement terms, and federation voting laws apply when members join or leave ongoing wars.
* **Trade Policy:** Trade gained from Commercial Pacts and Branch Offices is converted under the empire's Trade Policy like other Trade Value.
* **Nomad Options:** With their respective DLCs active, Nomads can pursue Cosmogenesis, Knights of the Toxic God, and a Nomad-compatible Storm Chasers Origin. Do not assume any of these is available without its DLC or chosen in the current campaign.
* **Nomad Shroud Accords:** Eligible Nomad empires can receive Arkship-appropriate rewards from the Composer of Strands, Whisperers in the Void, and End of the Cycle Greatest Patron accords.
* **Nomad Total Wars:** Nomads can gain defeated Arkships and Waystations after a Total War status quo and can take a percentage of Pops from planets they destroy. After a Total War status quo, Arkships defeated by settled empires are destroyed, and Nomad empires can themselves be defeated in Total Wars.
* **Nomad Purge Vassals:** Temporary Purge Vassals are special subjects that send resources through subject taxes and hand remaining Pops and resources to their overlord when their work ends. Assimilating overlords can receive eligible assimilated Pops rather than only purge outcomes.
* **Nomad Gateways:** Nomad empires can reactivate and own Gateway megastructures.
* **Nomad Opponents:** Nomad AI plans its economy, develops and upgrades Arkships, and places Waystations toward valuable systems. Treat assumptions about passive or poorly developed Nomad rivals with caution.
