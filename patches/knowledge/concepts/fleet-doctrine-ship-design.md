# Fleet Doctrine & Ship Design

<!-- Sources: patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Fleet Doctrine:** The Fleet Doctrine policy offers six doctrines that influence each ship class's tactical role in automated ship designs and designer auto-complete. Weapon and Defense Preference policies also guide automated designs and technology research. Check the empire's selected policies before recommending a specific auto-designed fleet composition. {since: 4.5}
