# Trade

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Market Currency:** Trade is the Galactic Market currency. Energy is an ordinary resource that can be bought and sold. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Trade Collection:** Trade is a standard stockpiled resource and is collected automatically. There is no current starbase collection-range, capital Trade Route, piracy, or patrol-fleet layer to configure. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Trade Logistics:** Planetary resource deficits and operating fleets consume Trade as logistics. Fleet Trade cost is lowest while docked and rises through friendly, neutral, and hostile locations; use actual campaign deficits and fleet locations before diagnosing the cause. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Trade Deficits:** A Trade deficit reduces Workforce effectiveness and increases Empire Size. Treat a persistent deficit as consequential, but only call one present when campaign data shows it. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Private Colony Ships and Slave Market:** Private colony ships and slave-market purchases use Trade rather than Energy. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Subsidy Edicts:** Production subsidy edicts use Efficiency bonuses with Trade upkeep. Their affordability depends on Trade, not only Energy. {since: 4.4.6}
