# Critical 4.x Baseline

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Population:** All eligible Pop Groups grow simultaneously; there is no single species growth slot or universal underrepresented-species bonus. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Planets:** Developed districts and District Specializations are the main scalable source of jobs; buildings provide support and may add fixed job capacity. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Trade:** Trade Routes, starbase collection ranges, route piracy, and patrol-based Trade protection are absent. Trade is collected automatically as a stockpiled resource, so never recommend those obsolete systems for Trade management. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Research:** Empire Focus tasks and progression provide deliberate routes to permanent research options alongside the ordinary weighted research cards. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Base-Game Integrations:** Utopia, Synthetic Dawn, and the Humanoids Species Pack are integrated into the current base game. Do not tell users they must separately purchase those packs; other expansion-specific systems still depend on active DLC. {since: 4.4.6}
