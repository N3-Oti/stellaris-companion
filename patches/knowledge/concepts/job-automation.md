# Job Automation

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Job Automation:** Job automation is planet-wide by job type. Automating Technicians applies to eligible Technician jobs across the planet, not only those from Generator Districts. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Automation Limits:** Automated jobs can use a Workforce limit. The presence of an automation building does not mean all eligible Workforce must be assigned to it. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Automation Economics:** Job-specific automation adds 4 Energy upkeep per 100 automated Worker or Simple Drone jobs, 8 per 100 Specialist or Complex Drone jobs, and 12 per 100 Elite jobs. In the standard unmodded economy, only Fallen Empire buildings automate Specialist or Complex Drone jobs; verify actual buildings before assuming that capability. {since: 4.4.6}
