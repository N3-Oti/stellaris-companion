# Exploration & Science Ships

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md); patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Science Ships:** Scientific Arkships can Survey and Deep Scan, but ordinary Science Ships are still required for anomalies, archaeology sites, and Astral Rifts. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Idle Automation:** Automated Science Ships that temporarily have no reachable task can remain automated and resume when new work appears. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Hostile Avoidance:** Automated ships retain automation and their current construction, research, exploration, or special-order task when avoiding hostile fleets. Temporary evasion does not require rebuilding the queue. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Explore vs Research:** Automation state does not prove that every anomaly, archaeology site, special project, or Astral Rift can be completed by that ship type. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Deep Scanning:** Eligible Deep Scans can reveal anomalies and archaeology sites at their intended 7% and 3% chances. Scientific Arkships gain +5% Deep Scanning Success Chance per Tier. {since: 4.5}
