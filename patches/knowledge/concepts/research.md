# Research

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Ordinary Research Remains:** Normal prerequisite and weighted research choices still operate. Do not claim that Empire Focus replaces the ordinary research-card system or immediately unlocks every technology in a theme. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Research Economy:** Research jobs operate on a lower-output scale paired with lower technology costs, while space-based research deposits are relatively more valuable. Do not import pre-4.3 researcher-output or technology-cost benchmarks. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Research Policies:** Technological Ascendancy unlocks policies that increase draw weight for selected technology categories. Separate Research Restriction policies can greatly reduce dangerous-technology draw chance, which is useful with automated research. {since: 4.4.6}
