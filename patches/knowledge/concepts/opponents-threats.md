# Opponents & Threats

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md); patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Dormant Powers:** A dormant Fallen Empire, living leviathan, marauder, or special contact is not automatically an immediate military threat. Look for hostility, awakening, borders, path obstruction, or other campaign evidence. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Fallen-Empire Scaling:** Fallen and Awakened Empire strength can vary with difficulty and galaxy settings. Do not assume one fixed fleet-power baseline. {since: 4.4.6}

- **AI Fleets:** AI fleet composition follows active fleet doctrine and weapon preference rather than fixed personality and civic ship ratios. Avoid predicting an opponent's composition from its personality alone. {since: 4.5}

- **Nomad Opponents:** Nomad AI plans its economy, develops and upgrades Arkships, and places Waystations toward valuable systems. Treat assumptions about passive or poorly developed Nomad rivals with caution. {since: 4.5}
