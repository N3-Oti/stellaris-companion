# Galaxy Settings

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Resource Settings:** Resource Abundance ranges from Scarce to x5 in 0.25 increments and defaults to x2. Deposit density should not be judged against a universal baseline when the setting is unknown. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Galaxy Scaling:** Advanced galaxy settings can scale Naval Capacity and Command Limit. Fleet-size advice should not assume default sliders when the settings are absent. {since: 4.4.6}
