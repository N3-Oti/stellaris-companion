# Colonies

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Colony Establishment:** A colony becomes established at 100 population. At the base monthly colony growth of 3, establishment from zero takes about 34 months. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Developing Colonies:** Before establishment completes, the colony is in its colonization phase rather than operating as a mature planet: it has no normal planetary production or ordinary Pop Growth. Modifiers, migration, bombardment, DLC, or mods can change its observed progress and vulnerability. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Underpopulated Colonies:** Eligible population is distributed across capped jobs, with designation, traits, and efficiency influencing assignment. Avoid assuming jobs fill in a fixed visible order. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Designations:** Colony designations improve the efficiency of their relevant jobs. Planetary Ascension increases a designation's effect by 10% per tier. {since: 4.4.6}
