# Infernals

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Infernals Systems:** With Infernals active, Volcanic Worlds, Thermophile and Volcanic Habitability traits, the Cosmic Dawn and Red Giant origins, and the Galactic Hyperthermia player Crisis path are valid current systems. Treat their presence as DLC-dependent rather than a mod or malformed save. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Galactic Hyperthermia:** Galactic Hyperthermia is a player Crisis path associated with Entropy. Its existence is not evidence that an empire selected or advanced it; require campaign evidence before narrating that progression. {since: 4.4.6}
