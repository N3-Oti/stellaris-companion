# Waystations

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Waystations:** Waystations harvest systems without normal ownership. Route quality, access, collection cadence, and network connectivity matter more than conventional chokepoint ownership alone. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Waylines:** Adjacent Waystations form Waylines and propagate network benefits. Do not double-count overlapping Waystation or Wayline effects. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Waystation Fauna:** Space fauna disable Waystations instead of destroying them. A Waystation can return when the fauna leaves or is defeated. {since: 4.4.6}
