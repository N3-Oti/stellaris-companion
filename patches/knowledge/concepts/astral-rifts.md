# Astral Rifts

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Dimensional Fleet:** The Dimensional Fleet Astral Action creates small ships equal to completed Astral Rifts and large ships equal to half that count, rounded down. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Astral Rifts:** Automated Science Ships return normally after exploring Astral Rifts. Do not warn that successful automated Rift exploration permanently strands the ship or Scientist. {since: 4.4.6}
