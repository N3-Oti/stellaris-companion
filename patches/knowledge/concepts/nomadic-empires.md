# Nomadic Empires

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md); patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433); patches/4.5.1.md (stable 4.5.1 hotfix notes) -->

- **Nomadic Structure:** Nomadic empires are not ordinary settled empires with an unusual authority. Evaluate them through Arkships, Waystations, Waylines, contracts, logistics, and reserves rather than normal border and colony-count expectations. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Settling and Embarking:** Nomads can settle after unlocking the relevant path, and eligible settled empires can embark into Nomad life. Embarking converts the strategic loop and can carry assault armies rather than destroying them. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Nomad Borders:** Nomads can often traverse closed borders until diplomatic or military action expels them. Treat access as conditional rather than normal territorial ownership. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Nomad Megastructures:** Nomads can build a megastructure in a system containing one of their Waystations even if the system is unsurveyed. Resource-producing megastructures require a Waystation for collection and should not be double-counted. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Nomad Options:** With their respective DLCs active, Nomads can pursue Cosmogenesis, Knights of the Toxic God, and a Nomad-compatible Storm Chasers Origin. Do not assume any of these is available without its DLC or chosen in the current campaign. {since: 4.5}

- **Nomad Gateways:** Nomad empires can reactivate and own Gateway megastructures. {since: 4.5}

- **Nomad Synaptic Lathes:** A Synaptic Lathe that Nomads build in another empire's system remains owned by the Nomads rather than transferring to the Starbase owner. {since: 4.5.1}
