# Fleet Logistics

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Logistics Fleet Upkeep:** The logistics-location multiplier is 0 while docked, 0.5 in friendly space, 1 in neutral space, and 2 in hostile space. {since: 4.4.6}

- **MIA Duration:** Fleets normally remain Missing in Action for no more than 10 years, with special exceptions such as phased, eaten, or Astral Rift fleets. {since: 4.4.6}
