# Pop Groups

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md); patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Pop Groups:** Pops are grouped for calculation by Species, Stratum or category, Ethics, and, where applicable, Faction. A Pop Group can supply Workforce to more than one job, and differences within a group are represented through its aggregate values. {since: 4.4.6, until: 4.5}

- **Pop Groups:** Groups track percentages of their Pops in each applicable Ethic and Faction rather than splitting into separate groups for those memberships. Do not infer ideological headcounts from the number of Pop Groups. {since: 4.5}
