# Cybernetics

<!-- Sources: patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Cyberization:** Cybernetic Pops gain 10% Job Efficiency from the Cybernetic species trait. The Limited Cybernetic species trait is absent. Under the Limited Cyberization policy, Cybernetic Spiritualist Pops do not benefit from Cybernetic Job Efficiency; the Limited Cyborg leader trait remains. {since: 4.5}

- **Pilot Project Bonuses:** Pilot Project bonuses grant +15% to Elites, +7.5% to Specialists, +10% to Workers, and +5% to all pops. {since: 4.5.2}
