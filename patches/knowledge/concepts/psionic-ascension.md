# Psionic Ascension

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md); patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Ascension Flow:** With Shadows of the Shroud active, Psionic Ascension proceeds through an Ascension Situation whose decisions influence Attunement and culminate in the first Delve after breaching the Shroud. Do not reduce the current path to the old sequence of perks and random Shroud visits. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Shroud Panel and Attunement:** The Shroud Panel tracks alignment with Patron domains. Deeds, time-sensitive Callings, Envoys, and Delving can affect Attunement, which in turn affects access and outcomes. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Accords and Covenants:** Attunement unlocks passive Accords. A Patron may offer a Covenant that unlocks stronger active powers with costs and cooldowns; eligible empires can instead reject Covenants and forge their own path. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Psionic Auras and Seals:** Psionic populations can project Patron-linked Auras from colonies. Their reach and strength depend on progression, and Shroud Seals or opposing Auras provide counterplay. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Gestalt Paths:** Hive Minds and Machine Intelligences can follow Psionic Ascension when the relevant DLC and path requirements are met. Do not assume all Gestalts are categorically excluded. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Zro Deficit:** A Zro deficit cancels Zro edicts, reduces psionic-pop bonus Workforce by 75% and psionic weapon damage by 50%, makes Shroud access materially slower or more expensive, and can lead to Shroud Incursions. Only diagnose this when the save reports a deficit. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Nomad Shroud Accords:** Eligible Nomad empires can receive Arkship-appropriate rewards from the Composer of Strands, Whisperers in the Void, and End of the Cycle Greatest Patron accords. {since: 4.5}
