# Ground Warfare

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Ground Warfare:** A normal species generally supports one pop-limited Assault Army per 100 pops, while some special army types are explicitly not limited by pops. Army counts and defensive-army sources are lower than older expectations, and orbital bombardment is stronger, so avoid importing pre-4.x benchmarks. {since: 4.4.6}
