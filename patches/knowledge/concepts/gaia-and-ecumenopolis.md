# Gaia and Ecumenopolis

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Gaia and Ecumenopolis Growth:** Gaia Worlds provide +15% Pop Growth and universal habitability. Ecumenopolises do not provide a Pop Growth bonus; their value is industrial capacity and job density. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Ecumenopolis Investment:** The Arcology Project and Ecumenopolis districts consume Alloys rather than Minerals, making conversion a major Alloy commitment. {since: 4.4.6}
