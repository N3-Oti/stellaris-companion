# Factions & Ethics

<!-- Sources: patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Faction Movement:** Ethics and Faction membership can shift frequently. Faction attraction responds to promoting or suppressing that Faction, and Factions with the same Ethics can be promoted or suppressed separately. {since: 4.5}

- **Faction Attraction:** Ethic attraction contributes to Faction growth; Pops have no inherent preference for their current Faction when choosing a Faction. Progressive, Prosperity, and Totalitarian attraction depends on citizenship rights rather than species alone. {since: 4.5}
