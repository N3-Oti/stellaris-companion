# Ship Combat

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md); patches/4.5.1.md (stable 4.5.1 hotfix notes) -->

- **Defensive Components:** Higher-tier armor and shields can add hardening. Defense analysis should account for bypass and hardening, not only raw hit points. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Crystal Plating:** Crystal hull plating adds flat damage reduction as well as Hull, making it more effective against repeated small hits than raw Hull alone suggests. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Siege Computers:** Siege Combat Computers are the torpedo-line combat computers and emphasize anti-starbase and bombardment roles. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Combat Disengagement:** Ships do not automatically disengage merely because they have not participated in combat for 15 days. {since: 4.5.1}
