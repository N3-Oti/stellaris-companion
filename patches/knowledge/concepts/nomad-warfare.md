# Nomad Warfare

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md); patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Arkship Warfare:** Arkships are disabled through hull damage rather than invaded like planets. Crises can destroy them, and Nomad-specific wars can transfer Arkships or Waystations. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Nomad Total Wars:** Nomads can gain defeated Arkships and Waystations after a Total War status quo and can take a percentage of Pops from planets they destroy. After a Total War status quo, Arkships defeated by settled empires are destroyed, and Nomad empires can themselves be defeated in Total Wars. {since: 4.5}

- **Nomad Purge Vassals:** Temporary Purge Vassals are special subjects that send resources through subject taxes and hand remaining Pops and resources to their overlord when their work ends. Assimilating overlords can receive eligible assimilated Pops rather than only purge outcomes. {since: 4.5}
