# Defender of the Galaxy

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Defender of the Galaxy:** With the Nomads expansion active, Defender of the Galaxy is a five-level Ambition advanced through cooperation, special projects, Custodianship, and an existential Crisis. It can unlock GDF and Paladin options and has a victory route based on defeating two endgame Crises before abdicating Custodianship. {since: 4.4.6}
