# Endgame Crises

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Multiple Crises:** When galaxy settings allow multiple endgame crises, another can become eligible 25 years after a previous crisis appears. This rule is not evidence that another crisis has already spawned. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Cosmogenesis:** Late Cosmogenesis activity can provoke direct Fallen Empire opposition. Do not narrate or predict that opposition as present unless campaign evidence supports it. {since: 4.4.6}
