# Consecrated Worlds

<!-- Sources: patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Consecrated Worlds:** Eligible worlds depend on an empire's origin and traits. Consecrating one, two, or three worlds grants deterministic empire-wide bonuses of +10%, +20%, or +40% to Unity, Amenities, and Spiritualist Ethics Attraction. {since: 4.5}
