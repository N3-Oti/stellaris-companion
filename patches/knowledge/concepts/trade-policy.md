# Trade Policy

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md); patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Trade Policy:** Trade Policy allocates monthly Trade production between retained Trade and the policy's other output resources. The default policy retains half as Trade and converts half to Energy; other available policies use different outputs. Income, conversion, and logistics expenses together determine the net Trade balance. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Trade Policy:** Trade gained from Commercial Pacts and Branch Offices is converted under the empire's Trade Policy like other Trade Value. {since: 4.5}
