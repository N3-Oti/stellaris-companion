# Districts & Buildings

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **District Development:** Districts provide jobs and housing according to their development. Productive capacity therefore depends on developed districts as well as available Workforce. {since: 4.4.6}

- **District Specializations:** District Specializations determine or add the job types provided by their districts and can constrain which buildings belong in that specialization. They are a primary tool for shaping a planet's output. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Specialization Slots:** Normal City Districts can support two specializations, while Generator, Mining, and Farming Districts support one each. Selecting a specialization opens three building slots, subject to technologies and special planet rules. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Buildings:** Buildings commonly support or modify jobs associated with their District Specialization, and some also provide a fixed number of jobs. Treat them as one layer of production alongside district development, specializations, available Workforce, upkeep, and building-slot opportunity cost. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Alloy Production:** Heavy Industry, also called the Foundry specialization in mechanic descriptions, adds Metallurgist jobs to developed City Districts. Tier I, II, and III Alloy Foundry buildings add 200, 400, and 600 Metallurgist job capacity respectively, equivalent to 2, 4, and 6 normally displayed jobs. They can supplement the specialization; check Workforce, inputs, upkeep, and competing building needs before recommending that every slot be filled with them. {since: 4.4.6}
