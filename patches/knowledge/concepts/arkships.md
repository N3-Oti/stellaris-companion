# Arkships

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Arkships:** Arkships are mobile capitals that combine colony, shipyard, and warship roles. Military, Scientific, and Civilian Arkships have distinct functions and can progress through tiers. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Habitability:** Arkships have inherent 40% habitability. Living Space Modules add 5% habitability and one district slot, with Exotic Gas cost and upkeep. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Colony Modifiers:** Planet-class, governor, colony, and scripted colony modifiers can apply to Arkship colonies. Do not assume Arkships ignore colony-side effects. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Stellar Engine:** A Tier III Arkship can equip the Stellar Engine Spinal Mount. Dark Matter ignition improves an existing star's Energy or Physics output, or turns a gas giant into a resource-rich protostar while absorbing its moons; the brighter system also suffers a habitability-ceiling penalty. Settled empires can issue Stellar Ignition contracts to a capable Nomad. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Weapon District Upkeep:** Arkship Laser Capacitors, Flight Decks, Ammunition Lines, and Missile Silos add strategic-resource upkeep to Soldiers. Include that upkeep when recommending weapon-focused Arkship districts. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Aura Cooldown:** After receiving a maximum-strength Arkship Aura effect, an Arkship has a 10-year cooldown before it can benefit that way again. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Arkship Exploration:** Arkships can use Explore automation to navigate toward Points of Interest. {since: 4.4.6}
