# Stellar Cannon

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md); patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Stellar Cannon:** With Nomads active, the Stellar Cannon is an offensive megastructure built around a neutron star or pulsar without requiring an Ascension Perk; generic Waystation access does not replace that star requirement. It charges for about 140 days, and every shot spends the owner's entire Energy stockpile and damages ships according to charge and tier. Spending at least 10,000 Energy additionally enables 10%, 25%, or 50% colony devastation by tier; 10,000 is not the minimum needed to fire. A Tier III shot over 50,000 can make unshielded planets drier, while Planetary Shields sharply reduce or block planetary effects. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Stellar Cannon:** The Stellar Cannon is a Tier 3 technology requiring Wormhole Stabilization and UV Lasers. Do not assume access merely from having a suitable star or Nomads DLC. {since: 4.5}
