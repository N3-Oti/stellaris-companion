# Population Scale

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

- **Population Scale:** Stellaris 4.x population is intentionally measured in the thousands. A large population value is not evidence of a broken save or a legacy one-pop-per-job economy. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Population Health:** Do not diagnose overpopulation, unemployment, or economic weakness from total population alone. Use the supplied planet, workforce, civilian, housing, stability, and resource evidence. {since: 4.4.6}
