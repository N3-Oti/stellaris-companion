# Federations

<!-- Sources: patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Federation Economic Laws:** Federations can grant member empires free automatic Research Agreements and Commercial Pacts, or Wayline Pacts when Nomads is active. Trade League members receive free automatic Commercial and, with Nomads, Wayline Pacts. Federation members can also form direct Commercial Pacts; non-Trade League members keep existing pacts when joining, while Trade Leagues replace member-to-member pacts with free federation pacts. Account for benefits even when no separately negotiated agreement was made. {since: 4.5}
