# Resource Deficits

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md); patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Living Metal Deficit:** A Living Metal deficit applies a severe job-output penalty. Only diagnose it when the save reports the deficit. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Biological Ship Deficits:** Biological ships are affected by Food deficits rather than Energy deficits. {since: 4.5}
