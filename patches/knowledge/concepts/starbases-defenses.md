# Starbases & Defenses

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md); patches/4.5.md (stable 4.5 release notes, Dev Diary #433) -->

- **Eternal Vigilance:** Eternal Vigilance does not create free defense platforms. It raises defense-platform capacity and strengthens home-territory defenses. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Starbase Preferences:** Starbases have separate Weapon and Defense Preference policies. Starbase, Orbital Ring, and Waystation weapon modules are weapon-type-specific, unlocked by matching weapon technology, and upgrade to the latest available tier. {since: 4.5}

- **Station Loadouts:** Autocannons, Disruptors, and other short-range weapons cannot be equipped on Starbases, Orbital Rings, or Deep Space Citadels. Automated stations fit armor in locations where shields are negated. {since: 4.5}
