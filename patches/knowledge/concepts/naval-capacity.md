# Naval Capacity

<!-- Sources: patches/snapshots/4.4.6.md (audit: patches/audits/4.4.6.md) -->

| Ship class | Normal naval-capacity use |
| --- | ---: |
| Corvette | 5 |
| Frigate | 8 |
| Destroyer | 10 |
| Cruiser | 20 |
| Battleship | 40 |
| Titan / Bio-Titan | 80 |
| Juggernaut / Colossus | 100 |
{since: 4.4.6}

- **Fleet Scale:** Current ships use substantially more Naval Capacity than older versions. For example, 100 free Naval Capacity fits 20 normal Corvettes before modifiers or special exceptions. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Anchorages:** A normal Anchorage adds 5 Naval Capacity and costs 4 Trade plus 1 Energy in upkeep. A Naval Logistics Office adds another 3 Naval Capacity per Anchorage, with 2 additional Trade upkeep per Anchorage, and has its own Energy upkeep. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Capacity Evidence:** Current usage is not the capacity ceiling. Do not say an empire is over or under its cap unless the supplied save data supports both values with sufficient confidence. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Over-Cap Fleets:** Exceeding Naval Capacity increases ship upkeep. Do not claim the penalty is active merely because fleet usage looks large. {since: 4.4.6}

- **Soldiers:** Soldiers, Battle Thralls, and Warrior Drones provide 4 Naval Capacity for 1 Alloy upkeep. Normal Soldiers are Specialists and gestalt Soldier equivalents are Complex Drones. {since: 4.4.6}
